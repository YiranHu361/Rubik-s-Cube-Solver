Photograph a scrambled cube from one corner, then from the opposite corner. That covers all six faces. The page reads the 54 stickers, checks they make a real cube, and finds a solution of about 20 turns with a two-phase search running in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

Sample photos are synthetic renders, marked as such. Replace them with your own.

Every colour must appear exactly nine times, each corner and edge piece exactly once, and the twists and flips must add up. The message above says which rule fails.
