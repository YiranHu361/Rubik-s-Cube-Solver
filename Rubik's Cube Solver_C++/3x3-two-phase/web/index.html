STEP 2 OF 3

Check the stickers

The top face of photo 1 is called U; its other two faces are R and F. Pick a colour, then click any sticker the camera got wrong.

Add two photos, or use the sample photos.

Every colour must appear exactly nine times, each corner and edge piece exactly once, and the twists and flips must add up. The message above says which rule fails.

Read with AI sends the two photos to Claude, which reads the 54 stickers from any angle, no outline needed. About two cents per read; only the photos leave your device, and only when you press it.
